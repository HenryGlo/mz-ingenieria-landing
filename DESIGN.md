# mz-ingenieria — independent concept
Editorial marketing site in Spanish. Source of business facts: user-provided prospect table. No invented results, certifications, testimonials or prices.
Distinctive direction: midnight technical grid, electric gradient typography, dimensional cabinet image and translucent technical note
Runtime token owner: body theme selectors and final CSS overrides in index.html. Motion: entrance, slow ambient animation, scroll reveals with prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
Interaction: native labeled select and textarea; prepares a WhatsApp message, never auto-sends or persists visitor data. Native accessible FAQ details.
Images generated with built-in image generation; fictional spaces and people, labeled throughout.

Hero revision: industrial editorial light palette, cobalt ink and orange accents, interactive schematic routing project/supply/advice inquiry. No decorative dark-gradient cards. Custom horizontal service list, documentary image panel and project briefing sheet. Route selection updates form. Diagram is conceptual customer routing, not electrical instructions.
